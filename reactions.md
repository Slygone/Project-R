## Rules for Reactions.

- Marks left from attacks are consumed to gain reaction. 
- Marks left from reactions are separate status.
- 



## Mono reaction. Marks needed (6) of a single mark
Fire-Fire -> Deals flat damage and applies **Ignite** for 2 turns (refreshed when re-applied). Ignite deals x damage per turn. If the enemy already has a DoT, this reaction deals +y% damage.
Ice-Ice -> Player gains Crit Rating 10% and Crit Damage 20% for the next turn 
Lightning-Lightning -> Deals flat damage and gains 2 extra max AP this and next turn. (when gaining AP is also restored 2 AP if user has 0 AP when procking reaction they gain 2 max ap and they are usable) 
Rock-Rock -> Applies shield to the player. Increases rock damage by 25% for the next 2 turns. 
Water-Water -> Deals damage and leaves mark on enemy for 2 turns (Refreshed when re-applied). For the next 2 turns when the enemy attcks the player the player heals for 1% of max hp. Once per turn. 
Wind-Wind -> Deals flat damage and reduces resistances by 40% for 2 turns.

## Dual Reaction. Marks needed of element A (3) and element B (3). 
Fire-Ice ->  Deals large amount of damage flat to the enemy. can crit 
Fire-Lightning -> Deals single target damage to the main target and area of effect damage to the surrounding enemies. 
Fire-Rock -> Leaves fire dot on the enemy stacking up to 3 dots. Each dot deals x amount of damage per turn for 2 turns.
Fire-Water -> Deals damage to the enemy and applies weak 50% to the enemy for 2 turns
Fire-Wind -> Deals damage to the enemy and lowers resistances to fire and wind by 25% for 2 turns

Ice-Lightning -> Ignores resistances when dealing reaction damage. Deals flat damage.
Ice-Rock -> Deals flat damage and applies Shatter for 2 turns. While Shatter is active, the enemy accumulates damage taken. When accumulated damage reaches X, Shatter pops: deal bonus flat damage and remove Shatter.
Ice-Water -> Freezes the enemy for one turn.
Ice-Wind -> deals flat damage and reduces resistances to ice and wind by 25% for 2 turns

Lightning-Rock -> Gain Reflective Armor for next turn: when hit, reflect 30% of damage taken back to the attacker. Also reduce damage taken by 10%.
Lightning-Water -> Leaves a mark on the enemy for 2 turns(Refeshed when re-applied). Each time the enemy is attacked mark is triggered dealing damage. Stacks up to 3 times making the damage taken 3 x stronger. 
Lightning-Wind -> deals flat damage and reduces resistances to lightning and wind by 25% for 2 turns

Rock-Water -> Applies mark on the enemy for 2 turns (refreshed when re-applied). At 3 marks, consume marks: deal damage and apply Stun 1 turn. 
Rock-Wind -> deals flat damage and reduces resistances to rock and wind by 25% for 2 turns

Water-Wind -> deals flat damage and reduces resistances to water and wind by 25% for 2 turns
