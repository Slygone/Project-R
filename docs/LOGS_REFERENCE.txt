LOG REFERENCE

This document lists the primary Debug.Log outputs, their meaning, and when they fire. Use it to quickly filter logs while debugging and to standardize future log messages.

Legend
- Tag: The prefix inside square brackets (e.g., [CombatLog])
- When: The event/state that triggers the log
- Fields: Key variables included in the message
- Example: Representative log line (placeholders in <>).


1) [CombatLog] EnemyAttack
- When: Each enemy attack after damage is rolled and resistance applied, just before showing QTE.
- Fields: Attacker, Element, Rolled, ResistTotal, ResistBonusFrom, Final
- Example: [CombatLog] EnemyAttack | Attacker=<Goblin> | Element=<Fire> | Rolled=<12> | ResistTotal=<30>% | ResistBonusFrom=<A> | Final=<8>
- Source: CombatManager.ProcessNextEnemyAttack()

2) [CombatManager] <enemy line>
- When: Enemy attack summary with element and rolled vs final damage, human-readable.
- Fields: Name, Element, Final, Rolled, Range
- Example: [CombatManager] Goblin (Fire) attacks for 8 damage (rolled: 12, range: 10-14)
- Source: CombatManager.ProcessNextEnemyAttack()

3) [CombatLog] DefensiveQTE
- When: After a Defensive QTE completes and healing is applied.
- Fields: Attacker, QTE, Healed
- Example: [CombatLog] DefensiveQTE | Attacker=<Goblin> | QTE=<Good> | Healed=<4>
- Source: CombatManager.OnDefensiveQTEComplete()

4) [DefensiveQTE] Result
- When: Player presses to resolve the Defensive QTE. Shows zone hit.
- Fields: Result, position
- Example: [DefensiveQTE] Result: Perfect (position: 0.50)
- Source: DefensiveQTE.ResolveQTE()

5) [CombatLog] ReactionQTEComplete
- When: Elemental Reaction QTE (offensive) resolves to determine QTEMultiplier. No healing.
- Fields: Result, QTEMultiplier
- Example: [CombatLog] ReactionQTEComplete | Result=<Good> | QTEMultiplier=<1.2>x
- Source: CombatManager.OnQTEComplete()

6) [CombatLog] PlayerAttack
- When: Player performs a basic attack (no reaction flow), after resistance applied.
- Fields: Element, InfusedFrom, RolledDmg, FinalDmg, Crit, Reaction, Resist, Target
- Example: [CombatLog] PlayerAttack | Element=<Water> | InfusedFrom=<A> | RolledDmg=<15> | FinalDmg=<12> | Crit=<False> | Reaction=<1.0>x | Resist=<20>% | Target=<Goblin>
- Source: CombatManager.ExecuteAttack()

7) [CombatLog] PlayerAttackReaction
- When: Player attack with an Elemental Reaction and QTE, after all multipliers and resistance.
- Fields: Reaction, DirectBase, QTEMult, Crit, CritMult, ReactionBonus, FinalDamage, Element, InfusedFrom, Resist, Target
- Example: [CombatLog] PlayerAttackReaction | Reaction=<Steam> | DirectBase=<22> | QTEMult=<1.2>x | Crit=<True> | CritMult=<1.5>x | ReactionBonus=<5> | FinalDamage=<28> | Element=<Water> | InfusedFrom=<A> | Resist=<10>% | Target=<Goblin>
- Source: CombatManager.ExecuteAttackWithReaction()

8) [CombatLog] PlayerSkill
- When: Player uses a skill without reaction QTE.
- Fields: Skill, Element, InfusedFrom, Damage, Crit, Reaction, Resist, Target
- Example: [CombatLog] PlayerSkill | Skill=<Bolt> | Element=<Fire> | InfusedFrom=<B> | Damage=<18> | Crit=<False> | Reaction=<1.0>x | Resist=<15>% | Target=<Goblin>
- Source: CombatManager.ExecuteSkill()

9) [CombatLog] PlayerSkillReaction
- When: Player uses a skill and triggers Reaction QTE (for multiplier) and reaction bonus is added.
- Fields: Skill, Reaction, DirectBase, QTEMult, Crit, CritMult, ReactionBonus, FinalDamage, Element, InfusedFrom, Resist, Target
- Example: [CombatLog] PlayerSkillReaction | Skill=<Ray> | Reaction=<Steam> | DirectBase=<24> | QTEMult=<1.1>x | Crit=<False> | CritMult=<1.0>x | ReactionBonus=<3> | FinalDamage=<27> | Element=<Water> | InfusedFrom=<B> | Resist=<5>% | Target=<Archer>
- Source: CombatManager.ExecuteSkillWithReaction()

10) [CombatManager] REACTION ATTACK / REACTION SKILL
- When: Human-readable summaries for reaction attacks/skills.
- Fields: Reaction name and elements, damage, target, QTE result
- Example: [CombatManager] REACTION ATTACK! Steam (Water+Fire) -> 28 damage to Goblin [QTE: Good]
- Source: CombatManager.ExecuteAttackWithReaction(), ExecuteSkillWithReaction()

11) [CombatUI] ShowDamageToEnemy / ShowDamageToPlayer
- When: UI feedback shows damage numbers and updates bars.
- Fields: Target name, amount, crit
- Example: [CombatUI] Enemy Goblin took 12 damage (CRIT!)
- Source: CombatUI.ShowDamageToEnemy(), ShowDamageToPlayer()

12) [Player] Took/Healed/Thresholds
- When: Player HP changes, thresholds (wounds) update, rest or stat changes.
- Fields: Amount, Health, MaxRecoverable, Wounds, threshold level
- Examples:
  - [Player] Took 10 damage. Health: 90/130 (MaxRecoverable: 100, Wounds: 1)
  - [Player] Healed 6. Health: 96/130 (Max recoverable: 100, Wounds: 1)
  - [Player] Wounds cleared! Can now heal to 130
  - [Player] Full rest: healed 52 HP, wounds cleared. Health: 130/130 (MaxRecoverable: 130)
- Source: Player.TakeDamage(), Heal(), ClearWounds(), FullRest()

13) [RestUI]
- When: Rest site actions (heal, ascend, upgrades).
- Fields: Heal amount, upgrade type/amount, XP spent
- Examples:
  - [RestUI] Player used full rest: 40% heal + wounds cleared
  - [RestUI] Bought +2 Damage for 3 XP (1/4)
- Source: RestUI.OnHealClicked(), On<X>UpgradeClicked()

14) [DataCache]
- When: Game data loads from CSVs.
- Fields: Counts of loaded datasets
- Example: [DataCache] Loaded: 16 enemies, 5 characters, ...
- Source: DataCache.LoadAll()

15) [GameManager]
- When: Global state changes, QTE result debug overlay, XP changes.
- Examples:
  - [GameManager] Set LastQTEResult: Reaction: Good
  - [GameManager] Run XP +3 (Elite)
- Source: GameManager helpers used across systems


Filtering Tips
- To see only combat summaries: filter for [CombatManager]
- To export data logs for balancing: filter for [CombatLog]
- To debug wound/threshold behavior: filter for [Player]
- To watch QTE flow: filter for [DefensiveQTE] and [CombatLog] DefensiveQTE

Proposed Structure Guidelines (for future edits)
- Always include a stable tag: [CombatLog], [CombatManager], [Player], etc.
- Use pipe-separated key=value or key fields (e.g., KeyA=<valueA>). Keep ordering consistent per message type.
- Avoid free prose in [CombatLog] lines; prefer parsable fields.
- Ensure every log has an unambiguous lifecycle point (When) in this file.
