# Characters Rework Templates

## Core Rules Applied
- All skills deal non-elemental base damage. Damage type is regular/physical unless a sigil enchantment is attached.
- Enchantments are bonus riders only. They improve mark setup or utility and do not redefine a skill into elemental damage.
- Skills 1-4 generate energy. Skill 5 always costs 100 energy.
- AP and cooldown brackets are enforced per slot for every character.
